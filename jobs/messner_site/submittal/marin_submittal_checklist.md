## D. What the plan checker will look for first

These are on the drawings, so plan check goes quickly:
- 2025 code references (R318/R320/R321/R324/R325, CWUIC 504.7.3).
- The **42" guard** (California, R321.1.2).
- The **R507.9.2** ties.
- The **6" vertical metal flashing** (CWUIC 504.7.3.1).
- The ledger flashing.
- The OSFM listing number on the decking callout.
- Safety-glazing marks.
- Existing vs. NEW items clearly clouded.
- The verification method for each concealed item.
- The engineer's stamp on every engineered sheet.

## E. Fees (the County fee schedule could not be retrieved; confirm at intake)

- **At Part A:** a **$100 non-refundable deposit** (check or card; cards add a **2.35%** service fee kept by the card processor).
- **Building & Safety fee:**
  - set by the CDA fee schedule from the project valuation;
  - the **application (plan review) portion, about 77%**, is due at submittal;
  - the **issuance portion, about 23%**, is due at issuance.
  - Source: the County permit portal's fee explanation, via search summary **[confirm]**.
- **Technology fee:** a percentage of the building permit fee (5% in MCC 19.04.032; reported raised to 6%) **[confirm]**.
- **Other reviews, if referred:**
  - **Planning (zoning) review fee**;
  - **SMFD fire review fee**.
- **As-built and investigation note.** Work done without a permit can carry extra charges:
  - MCC 19.04.029 (2013 text) allows a penalty of **2 to 4 times the permit fees**;
  - MCC 19.04.033 provides for permit costs, **investigation fees** and penalties.
  - The amount actually charged on a voluntary legalization is not published online. **Ask at intake** whether an investigation fee applies and how it is computed.
- **Third-party plan check** (optional): paid to the consultant. The older MCC 19.04.032 text allowed up to a 50% reduction of the County plan-review fee with pre-approval **[confirm]**.
- **Not County fees:** the engineer, the surveyor (if needed) and the corrective work (see the internal budget).

## G. Inspection sequence for an as-built deck

Schedule each inspection through the County's inspection request system as shown on the permit card **[confirm the method]**. Do not close anything up until it has been released.

1. **Site verification (investigation) inspection, before any corrective work.**
   - **Have open:**
     - deck boards lifted along the house wall at 3 places (both ends and the middle), showing the ledger bolts, washers, flashing and WRB;
     - **1 to 2 piers exposed** to the bottom of the footing, with a tape in place;
     - the fascia removed at 2 or 3 guard posts (brackets, fasteners, blocking);
     - the soffit opened at both deck ends (tension-tie anchorage, floor framing at the cantilever);
     - the brace ends accessible.
   - **Have ready:**
     - the engineer's observation letter;
     - the photo log;
     - the approved plans and the permit card;
     - a ladder;
     - the 4" and 4-3/8" spheres.
   - The inspector confirms what exists matches the plans and lists any additional exposure.
2. **Pre-pour (footing) inspection** for any NEW concrete: the stair landing pad, and any new pier or support. Forms and rebar in place; excavation to firm native soil.
3. **Framing and connector inspection** after the corrective structural work, before re-closing soffits, fascia or boards:
   - DTT2Z ties (both sides);
   - knee-brace bolts or screws;
   - post-cap screws or new caps;
   - H2.5AZ ties;
   - guard-post blocking and bolts;
   - stair supports;
   - any engineer's retrofit.
4. **Flashing inspection:** the ledger flashing and membrane, and the **6" vertical metal flashing** (CWUIC 504.7.3.1), before the siding and boards go back.
5. **Electrical rough**, if any wiring is added or exposed.
6. **Fire (SMFD) sign-off, if the County routes it:**
   - WUI decking listing on site;
   - under-deck area clear of combustibles;
   - defensible space.
7. **Final:**
   - guard heights (42" deck and landing; 34" or more stairs);
   - sphere tests;
   - glass marks visible;
   - handrail;
   - riser and tread uniformity;
   - stair-top light;
   - under-deck area clear;
   - the house-wide items Marin checks at final (MCC 19.14.020: smoke and CO alarms, water-conserving fixtures);
   - the engineer's final letter, if the plans require one.
