## Scope requested

1. **Review** PCG's drawings, as-built model and photos.
   - **One site visit** to coincide with the owner's exposure day: ledger boards lifted at 3 locations, fascia removed at 2 or 3 guard posts, soffit opened at the deck ends, 2 piers exposed to full depth, brace ends probed.
   - A brief field observation letter.
2. **Beams and gravity:**
   - the 6x12 beams (not in CRC Tables R507.5): bending, shear, deflection and bearing at splices;
   - posts (including any bending from the braces), caps, and joists (confirm against Table R507.6);
   - stair stringers, including the upper flight's notched center stringer;
   - the landing posts.
3. **Lateral system, both directions (seismic and wind):**
   - the deck-to-house load path and the tension ties (R507.9.2 minimum: two devices of 1,500 lb or more, within 24" of each end; only one found so far), including the house-side anchorage;
   - **the knee braces:** design end connections and include them in the lateral design, or confirm in writing that they can be removed;
   - post-base and footing overturning and uplift.
4. **Post-to-beam connection:**
   - confirm the existing caps are rated and fastened, or specify rated side-mount caps or a bolted plate detail;
   - splices over posts.
5. **Fascia-mounted glass guard.** ESR-4217 recognizes the IRX fascia bracket only on concrete and does not list glass infill. Please provide the post-to-rim connection for:
   - 200 lb at the top rail;
   - wind on the glass (ASCE 7-22 Ch. 29);
   - 50 lb infill;
   - both edge conditions (rim perpendicular to the joists, and end joist parallel), with blocking and roll restraint per R507.10.1.1. The IRX guide calls for 5/16" through-bolts and double blocking; the field has lags.
6. **Ledger on the cantilevered floor (R507.9.1.2):** check the cantilevered house floor framing for the ledger reaction and tie forces, with the wall and roof above. If it does not work, provide the simplest supplemental support, for example a beam and posts at the house line, so the deck is self-supporting vertically.
7. **Footings on slope:**
   - the R403.1.7 setback;
   - pier size and depth vs. load, from the exposures (presumptive 1,500 psf unless you require more);
   - any retrofit detail (collar or sister pier).
