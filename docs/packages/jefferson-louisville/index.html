T-400 · Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (7) Diamond Pier DP-50/50 (head + 4 x 50" pins, plugs, caps)
  • (1) Upgrade to Diamond Pier DP-75/63 at the shared main cover posts (roof + deck on one pier)

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin. Strip the existing deck first.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: FLUSH boxed 7-1/4" steel C W at 3'-6 1/2" from the reference wall, posts at 7 3/16" / 3'-0" / 8'-4" / 14'-1 3/16"; FLUSH boxed 7-1/4" steel C M at 11'-10 1/2" from the reference wall, posts at 15'-2" / 20'-8" / 26'-2".
  • Set 7 Diamond Pier heads level at grade on undisturbed soil and drive the 4 pins in each; caps on.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Pins fully driven, heads level
  • Photos before backfill

T-401 · Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

Check

  • Every bracket fastened: thru-bolts and #12 screws per the engineer
