How it's built step by step

Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (3) Simpson Titen HD 316SS 3/8x3 Screw Anchor
  • (9) Diamond Pier Deck Footing DP50
  • (9) Simpson Adjustable Post Base 6x6 - Zmax
  • (11) Quikrete 80# Concrete Mix

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin. Strip the existing deck first.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: DROP (2)2x10 SYP N at 1'-1 1/2" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-0 1/2" / 6'-11 1/2"; DROP (2)2x10 SYP N+S at 9'-10 1/2" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-0 1/2" / 8'-0" / 14'-11 1/2"; DROP (2)2x10 SYP L at 13'-10 1/2" from the reference wall, posts at 17'-0 1/2" / 18'-11 1/2".
  • Set 9 Diamond Pier heads level at grade on undisturbed soil and drive the 4 pins in each; caps on.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Pins fully driven, heads level
  • Photos before backfill

Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

  • (9) 6x6x8 #2 - GC
  • (3) Simpson Titen HD 316SS 3/8x3 Screw Anchor
  • (9) Simpson Adjustable Post Base 6x6 - Zmax

How

  • 6x6 posts on the bases; cut so the beam top lands at the joist bottom (drop beam) or the joist top (flush beam): DROP (2)2x10 SYP N posts 6'-0" / DROP (2)2x10 SYP N+S posts 6'-0" / DROP (2)2x10 SYP L posts 6'-0" — field-measure each.
  • Plumb both ways, brace, then the caps go on with the beams.

Check

  • Posts plumb to a string; tops on the beam line
  • No post in ground contact without a standoff

Step 4 · Beams

Goes in

  • (3) 2x6x12 #1 KDAT - GC
  • (1) 2x4x12 #1 KDAT - GC
  • (9) 6x6x8 #2 - GC
  • (3) Simpson Adjustable Post Cap 6x6 - Zmax
  • (4) Simpson 6x Post Cap Adjustable Zmax
  • (4) Simpson 6x Heavy Adjustable Post Cap - Zmax

How

  • DROP (2)2x10 SYP N: 7'-11" one piece, 2 posts @ 5'-11" max
  • DROP (2)2x10 SYP N+S: 15'-11" one piece, 3 posts @ 6'-11 1/2" max
  • DROP (2)2x10 SYP L: 3'-11" one piece, 2 posts @ 1'-11" max
  • Crown up, strung straight, caps screwed off with the SDS screws in the box.

Check

  • Beam top = joist bottom (drop) / joist top (flush)
  • Splices land on a post; caps fully fastened

Step 5 · Frame

Goes in

  • (27) 2x10x12 #1 True Frame Joist - GC
  • (10) 2x10x16 #1 True Frame Joist - GC
  • (3) 2x10x16 #1 True Frame Joist - GC (line 17)
  • (4) 2x10x20 #1 True Frame Joist - GC
  • (6) Nichigo G-Tape 3035 2"x65' Deck Flash Tape - Black w/ NO Liner
  • (2) Nichigo G-Tape 3035 4"x65' Deck Flash Tape - Black w/ NO Liner
  • (58) Simpson Hurricane Ties Z-Max H2.5AZ
  • (1) Nichigo G-Tape 3040 9"x65' Deck Flash Tape - Black w/ Liner

How

  • 2x10 ledger x 12'-0" total · LedgerLOK 2 rows 2" from top/bottom, staggered 12" OC, first 3" from each end · 13 fasteners
  • N: 7 joists @ 12" OC x 11'-6"; S: 7 joists @ 12" OC x 11'-7 1/2"; L: 3 joists @ 12" OC x 15'-7 1/2". Crown up, hangers at the ledger.
  • Blocking one row over the beam, staggered.
  • G-Tape 2" every joist, PF joist, blocking and the ledger top · 4" on both rims and the beam(s)

Check

  • Frame square: diagonals within 1/8"
  • Every hanger fully nailed; ledger fasteners per the schedule
  • Tape continuous; photos of the ledger, hangers and footings before decking

Step 6 · Decking

Goes in

  • (10) Azek Vintage 1x6x16 - Weathered Teak - Square
  • (27) Azek Vintage 1x6x20 - Weathered Teak - Square
  • (5) Azek Vintage (1/2x12x12') Fascia - Weathered Teak
  • (3) Cortex for TimberTech Vintage — 100 SF (screws + Weathered Teak plugs, setting tool)
  • (1) Cortex for TimberTech Vintage — 300 SF (screws + Weathered Teak plugs, setting tool)
  • (2) Cortex for TimberTech fascia — 30 LF (screws + Weathered Teak plugs)
  • (1) Camo 3" x #9 Premium Deck Screw ProTech 2-Tan (350 ct)

How

  • Cortex face screws with color-matched plugs: 2 per board at every joist and at both ends of every run, color-matched plugs (1442 field + 0 border/divider)
  • Borders first, then the field.
  • Rows per zone: N 25 · S 25 · L 34. Borders and dividers first, then the field rows from the outer edge toward the house.

Check

  • 3/16" gaps held
  • No cut end shows at the outer edge
  • Color: every board from the same run

Goes in

  • (3) Azek Impression Express 3x3x38" Horizontal Cable Post Kit, Corner - Black
  • (4) Azek Impression Express 3x3x38" Horizontal Cable Post Kit, Level End - Black

  • (10) Azek Impression Express 316ss Cable (20' x 1/8") & Hardware
  • (20) Azek Impression Express 316ss Cable (40' x 1/8") & Hardware

  • IRX 36" — 9 posts (3 CORNER, 4 END, 2 LINE), 7 bays at 3'-7" / 5'-9 1/2" / 7'-7" / 7'-9 1/2" CTC (8' kits cut to bay), posts on the divider lines, no bottom rail; HeadLOK 6" x 4 per post

  • Top of rail 36" above the decking; posts plumb
  • Cables tensioned / balusters under 4" clear
  • End of construction
