T-400 · Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (2) Diamond Pier DP-50/50 (head + 4 x 50" pins, plugs, caps)
  • (2) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin. Strip the existing deck first.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: DROP (2)4x10 DF#1 at 9'-2" from the reference wall, posts at 9" / 7'-3".
  • Set 2 Diamond Pier heads level at grade on undisturbed soil and drive the 4 pins in each; caps on.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Pins fully driven, heads level
  • Photos before backfill

T-401 · Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

  • (2) 6x6x10 Douglas Fir #1
  • (2) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt
  • (1) Sail hardware: 4 stainless turnbuckles (jaw-jaw), D-shackles, snap hooks, 2 house anchor plates with through-bolts / structural screws into the framing

How

  • 6x6 posts on the bases; cut so the beam top lands at the joist bottom (drop beam) or the joist top (flush beam): DROP (2)4x10 DF#1 posts 8'-0 1/16" — field-measure each.
  • Plumb both ways, brace, then the caps go on with the beams.

Check

  • Posts plumb to a string; tops on the beam line
  • No post in ground contact without a standoff

T-403 · Step 4 · Beams

Goes in

  • (2) 4x10x8 Douglas Fir #1
  • (2) 6x6x10 Douglas Fir #1
  • (2) Simpson CCQ column cap, 7" seat for the (2)4x10 beam on a 6x6 post (SDS screws incl.) — confirm SKU / engineer's equal
  • (2) 4x4x8 Douglas Fir #1 — knee braces
  • (4) 1/2" x 10" HDG hex bolt + nut + 2 washers — knee braces (brace to the 6x6 post)
  • (2) 1/2" x 12" HDG hex bolt + nut + 2 washers — knee braces (brace to the (2)4x beam)
  • (2) 1/2" x 6" HDG hex bolt + nut + 2 washers — knee braces (brace to the joist block)
  • (10) 1/2" x 8" HDG carriage bolt + nut + washer

How

  • DROP (2)4x10 DF#1: 7'-8" one piece, 2 posts @ 6'-6" max
  • Crown up, strung straight, caps screwed off with the SDS screws in the box.
  • Two-ply beam: through-bolt the plies with 1/2" HDG bolts, 2 rows 2" from top and bottom, staggered @ 24" OC, a pair 6" from each end — before it goes up.
  • Knee braces once the beam is capped: 4x4 at 45°, 2'-6" legs, one along the beam toward mid-span and one toward the house up to a 2x10 block between two joists (set the block with the frame) — 1/2" through-bolt each end, then pull the temporary bracing.

Check

  • Beam top = joist bottom (drop) / joist top (flush)
  • Splices land on a post; caps fully fastened
  • Every knee brace bolted both ends; posts still plumb
