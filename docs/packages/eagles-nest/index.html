T-400 · Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (3) Sonotube 20" x 12' (cut to length — 3 caissons per tube) — caissons 20" x 3'-6" + 6" above grade (frost 3'-4")
  • (120) Quikrete 80# concrete mix
  • (7) #4 rebar x 20' (4 verticals per caisson)
  • (7) #3 rebar x 20' (ties)
  • (8) 5/8" x 8" HDG anchor bolt w/ nut + washer (cast-in)
  • (8) Simpson 8x8 post base, BLACK powder-coat (Outdoor Accents APB88 or shop-coated ABU88Z)

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin. Strip the existing deck first.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: FLUSH 6x12 DF C at -5'-0" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-9 1/2" / 10'-9 11/16"; DROP 6x12 DF C+B+A at 4'-0" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-6 1/2" / 12'-7 3/16" / 19'-10 13/16" / 27'-2 3/8" / 40'-5 1/8" / 53'-7 7/8".
  • Auger 8 holes 20" x 3'-6" (frost 3'-4"), tube to 6" above grade, cage in, pour, set the 5/8" anchors wet. Cure 2 days.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Frost depth met
  • Photos before backfill

T-401 · Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

  • (8) 8x8x8 Douglas Fir #1 timber (S4S, end grain sealed)
  • (8) 5/8" x 8" HDG anchor bolt w/ nut + washer (cast-in)
  • (8) Simpson 8x8 post base, BLACK powder-coat (Outdoor Accents APB88 or shop-coated ABU88Z)
  • (1) End-grain sealer (Anchorseal class) — every cut

How
