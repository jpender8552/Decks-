A new, larger deck off your back door: 8' along the house and 12' out, 10' above the backyard, in place of the existing one. Framed in TrueFrame lumber at 16" on center on a doubled 4x10 Douglas fir flush beam across the front and two 6x6 Douglas fir #1 posts with knee braces, on Diamond Pier foundations, with joist tape on every member. TimberTech Reserve in Driftwood with a single picture frame on the three open sides and CONCEALoc hidden fasteners, so no screw heads show. TimberTech Fulton rail in Black, steel posts set inside the rim. Two steel posts that double as the front corner posts of the railing, with house anchors, to hang and tension your square shade sail over the deck (you supply the sail). The house attachment is engineered.
