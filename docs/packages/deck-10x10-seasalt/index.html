Build set T-400 series · goes in · how · check

T-400 · Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (2) Diamond Pier DP-50/50 (head + 4 x 50" pins, plugs, caps)
  • (2) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: DROP 4x10 DF at 7'-5 1/4" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-6" / 8'-4 3/16".
  • Set 2 Diamond Pier heads level at grade on undisturbed soil and drive the 4 pins in each; caps on.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Pins fully driven, heads level
  • Photos before backfill

T-401 · Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

  • (2) 6x6x10 #2 GC
  • (2) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt

How

  • 6x6 posts on the bases; cut so the beam top lands at the joist bottom (drop beam) or the joist top (flush beam): DROP 4x10 DF posts 8'-2 1/16" — field-measure each.
  • Plumb both ways, brace, then the caps go on with the beams.

Check

  • Posts plumb to a string; tops on the beam line
  • No post in ground contact without a standoff

T-403 · Step 4 · Beams

Goes in

  • (1) 4x10x10 Douglas Fir #2
  • (2) 6x6x10 #2 GC
  • (2) Simpson BC46Z post cap 6x post / 4x beam

How

  • DROP 4x10 DF: 9'-8" one piece, 2 posts @ 6'-10 3/16" max
  • Crown up, strung straight, caps screwed off with the SDS screws in the box.

Check

  • Beam top = joist bottom (drop) / joist top (flush)
  • Splices land on a post; caps fully fastened

T-404 · Step 5 · Frame

Goes in

  • (15) 2x8x10 #1 True Frame Joist SYP GC
  • (1) 2x8x12 #1 True Frame Joist SYP GC
  • (16) Simpson LUS28Z face-mount hanger 2x8/2x10
  • (4) Simpson HUCQ28-2-SDS concealed-flange double 2x8 hanger
  • (1) Camo 9ga HDG hanger nail 3" 5 lb (~350)
  • (1) Camo 9ga HDG hanger nail 1-1/2" 5 lb (~650)
  • (1) FastenMaster LedgerLOK 3-5/8" 50 ct
  • (10) Simpson H2.5AZ hurricane tie (joists to the drop beam, hidden)
  • (1) Ledger flashing set: SA membrane 6"x75' + (2) 10' alum. Z + end dams
  • (2) Nichigo G-Tape 3035 2" x 65' black

How

  • 2x8 ledger x 9'-9" total · LedgerLOK 2 rows 2" from top/bottom, staggered 12" OC, first 3" from each end · 11 fasteners
  • A: 6 joists @ 16" OC x 9'-2 1/2". Crown up, hangers at the ledger.
  • Blocking one row over the beam, staggered.
  • G-Tape 2" every joist, PF joist, blocking and the ledger top · 4" on both rims and the beam(s)

Check

  • Frame square: diagonals within 1/8"
  • Every hanger fully nailed; ledger fasteners per the schedule
  • Tape continuous; photos of the ledger, hangers and footings before decking

T-405 · Step 6 · Decking

Goes in

  • (20) TimberTech Prime+ Sea Salt Gray 1x6x12 Grooved
  • (1) TimberTech Prime+ Sea Salt Gray 1x6x12 Square Edge — borders
  • (1) TimberTech Prime+ Sea Salt Gray 1x6x20 Square Edge — borders
  • (3) Deck skirt — HardieTrim 4/4 x 12 x 12' smooth ColorPlus (color TBD) (wraps the double rim, in place of the composite fascia)
  • (1) Color-matched trim screws for HardieTrim, 100 ct ((color TBD))
  • (1) ColorPlus touch-up kit, (color TBD)
  • (2) Camo EdgeClip 3/16" 410SS Black 90 ct

How

  • 100% hidden — Camo EdgeClip 3/16" at every joist in every gap (9 bearing points x 20 gaps = 180); board ends clipped on the PF joists, never screwed
  • Cap-Tor xd color-matched ONLY at: first row 1 per joist on the house edge · borders 2 per bearing point @ 16" · fascia 2 at each board end then alternate top/bottom every 12", pre-drilled
  • Rows per zone: A 20. Borders and breakers first, then the field rows from the outer edge toward the house.

Check

  • 3/16" gaps held
  • No cut end shows at the outer edge
  • Color: every board from the same run
