T-400 · Step 1 · Footings

Goes in

  • (3) Diamond Pier DP-50/50 (head + 4 x 50" pins, plugs, caps)
  • (3) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt

How

  • Call 811 and locate before any hole or pin. Strip the existing deck first.
  • Pull the beam lines from the house wall: DROP 6x12 DF S+A at 16'-1 3/16" from the reference wall, posts at 1'-0 1/2" / 9'-6 1/2" / 18'-0 1/2".
  • Set 3 Diamond Pier heads level at grade on undisturbed soil and drive the 4 pins in each; caps on.

Check

  • Footings on the layout within 1/2"; bearing on undisturbed soil
  • Pins fully driven, heads level
  • Photos before backfill

T-401 · Step 2 · Posts

Goes in

  • (1) 6x6x8 #2 GC
  • (3) Simpson ABA66Z adjustable post base 6x6 on the pier bolt

How

  • 6x6 posts on the bases; cut so the beam top lands at the joist bottom (drop beam) or the joist top (flush beam): DROP 6x12 DF S+A posts 6" — field-measure each.
  • Plumb both ways, brace, then the caps go on with the beams.

Check

  • Posts plumb to a string; tops on the beam line
  • No post in ground contact without a standoff

T-403 · Step 4 · Beams

How

  • Crown up, strung straight, caps screwed off with the SDS screws in the box.

Check

  • Beam top = joist bottom (drop) / joist top (flush)
  • Splices land on a post; caps fully fastened
