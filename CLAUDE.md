# GSX Deck Division: standing rules (read before any deck work)

These are Jade Pender's rules for GS Exterior Experts. They override manufacturer defaults and generic "common practice". Every agent brief MUST carry them. When a document disagrees, follow these rules and flag the conflict.

## Layout
- **No butt joints, ever.** Every field board is one continuous piece between borders and dividers. If a run is longer than the longest board, add a **divider board**.
- Use a **single-board picture frame** on every edge, with 45° miters, and **divider boards** between fields.
- Size the frame to the decking so every board is full width, with zero rips where possible.
- Every board end bears continuously:
  - a picture-frame joist about 1-1/2" inboard of the rim;
  - blocking under every divider and frame board.

## Fastening
- **All PVC** (TimberTech Advanced PVC: Harvest, Vintage, Landmark, ...): face-screw **every** board with **Cortex** screws and plugs. No hidden clips and no SIDELoc on PVC.
- **All composite** (TimberTech Reserve, Legacy, Terrain, Terrain+, Premier, ...):
  - **CONCEALoc** hidden clips on the grooved field;
  - **Cortex** face screws only on the square-shoulder borders, dividers, nosings and treads.
- **Scalloped composite** (Prime, Prime+): Cortex is not approved. Use the hidden clip in the GSX standards.
- **Face-screw counts on composite:**
  - first row: 1 per joist;
  - borders: 2 per bearing point;
  - fascia: 2 at each board end, then alternating top and bottom every 12", pre-drilled.

  Jade rejects heavier counts.

## Framing
- **Composite:** 16" on center perpendicular, with **one** blocking row over the beam. Two rows is a PVC habit.
- **PVC:** 12" on center.
- **Diagonal (45°) fields:** closer spacing, per TimberTech for each collection (verify and cite).
- **Joist tape:** 2" on every joist and on blocking; 4" on rims and beams.
- **Rims:** double front and side rims, never more plies.
  - Double rims hang in HUCQ210-2-SDS.
  - LUS hangers are for single joists only.
- **Ledger:**
  - LedgerLOK in 2 rows, staggered;
  - membrane behind;
  - aluminum Z-flash over;
  - end dams;
  - (4) DTT1Z lateral ties.
- The full framing, railing, footing and pricing standards are in the gsx-deck-docs skill: `references/standards.md`.

## Takeoffs and orders
- Every line has **NET** (the drawing count) and **ORDER** (the PO quantity), with a one-line reason wherever they differ.
- **Overage:**
  - field boards +2;
  - 1 spare border;
  - 1 spare fascia.

  No attic stock.
- No pricing in a takeoff. Jade prices; follow his pricing rules in the skill.

## Documents and visuals
- Write facts only, in American spelling, with no adjectives Jade didn't write.
- Use no permit, inspection or AHJ wording unless Jade asks for it.
- Use official GSX logos only.
- QA means looking at every rendered page and image.
- Send files individually, never zipped.
- Use our own renders only. Never put manufacturer photos in a deliverable; sampling them for color calibration is OK.
- Isometrics are right-handed. Every board has grain and its own tone.

## Working rules
- Read the job's facts file and these rules **before** briefing any agent, and paste the rules that apply into the brief.
- An auditor checks every output against these rules before anything goes to Jade.
- This repo is **public**:
  - never commit customer photos, personal or financial information, or private jobs;
  - Messner stays on its private local branch.
